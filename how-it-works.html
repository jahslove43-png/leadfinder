HOW IT WORKS

From targeting to an organized lead list.

LeadFinder is built around a simple campaign workflow: define the market, discover businesses, review signals, and export the results.

01

Choose your market

Select categories and keywords, then choose worldwide coverage or specific countries, regions/states, and cities.

02

Create a campaign

Give the campaign a name, set a daily target, choose a scan time and timezone, and optionally configure an email for operational notifications.

03

Run discovery

Run a scan manually or let an active scheduled campaign run when the required discovery provider, quota, and service availability are in place.

04

Review signals

LeadFinder records discovery results and distinguishes identified websites from records that need additional verification.

05

Manage leads

Review lead scores, campaign relationships, lead status, locations, and scan history from the dashboard.

06

Export results

Completed scans can produce CSV exports for use in your own sales or research workflow.

IMPORTANT

Coverage depends on the data providers.

Worldwide targeting is a campaign option, not a promise that every business on Earth will be discovered. Results depend on provider coverage, quotas, availability, location data, and verification evidence.
