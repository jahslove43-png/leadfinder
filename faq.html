FAQ

Questions before you start?

Here are the key product and usage questions to understand before creating a campaign.

What does LeadFinder do?

It lets you create targeted business-discovery campaigns, run scans, review results, manage leads, and export completed results.

Can I search worldwide?

Yes. Campaigns can be configured as worldwide or narrowed to selected countries, regions/states, and cities. Actual coverage depends on discovery providers, available data, quotas, and service availability.

Does “no website found” mean the business definitely has no website?

No. It is a discovery/verification result, not a guarantee. Results can be incomplete or outdated, so important prospects should be checked before outreach.

How often can scans run?

You can configure a campaign schedule. Actual execution can be affected by provider quotas, API availability, rate limits, errors, and account configuration.

Will LeadFinder contact businesses automatically?

The current product is focused on discovery, organization, exports, and notifications about scans. You remain responsible for deciding if, when, and how to contact a business.

Is my account data public?

Your account and campaign data are protected by authentication and database access controls. Avoid putting unnecessary sensitive information into campaign fields or exported files.

Can I export my results?

Yes. Completed scans can generate CSV exports that are available from the dashboard when the export process succeeds.

What can cause a scan to be partial or fail?

Provider quotas, rate limits, API outages, network errors, invalid configuration, or other service conditions can interrupt a scan.
