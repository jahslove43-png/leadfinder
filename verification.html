Data accuracy & verification

LeadFinder treats website-status information as a signal that should be interpreted in context.

WEBSITE_FOUNDA website was identified by the available discovery evidence.
NO_WEBSITE_FOUNDNo website was identified by the configured checks. This is not a guarantee that the business has no website.
NEEDS_VERIFICATIONThe available evidence is insufficient for a definitive conclusion.
VERIFICATION_FAILEDA verification attempt could not be completed successfully.

Verify important prospects

Business information can change. Before using a lead for a consequential business decision or outreach, independently verify the details that matter to you.

Provider limitations

Discovery coverage, location data, websites, phone numbers, and other fields can be incomplete, outdated, or unavailable. Provider quotas and technical failures can also affect scan completeness.
