ACCURACY MATTERS

We distinguish a signal from a fact.

A business without a visible website in a data source is not automatically proof that it has no website. LeadFinder uses explicit status states so uncertain results can be reviewed instead of being presented as guaranteed facts.

WEBSITE_FOUNDA website was identified by the discovery provider.
NO_WEBSITE_FOUNDNo website was found after the configured verification process.
NEEDS_VERIFICATIONThe available evidence is not enough to make a definitive determination.
VERIFICATION_FAILEDA verification attempt could not be completed successfully.

FAQ

Questions before you start?

What does LeadFinder do?

It lets you create targeted business-discovery campaigns, run scans, review results, manage leads, and export completed results.

Can I search worldwide?

Yes. Campaigns can be configured as worldwide or narrowed to selected countries, regions or states, and cities. Actual coverage depends on the discovery providers, available data, quotas, and service availability.

Does “no website found” mean the business definitely has no website?

No. It is a discovery/verification result, not a guarantee. Results can be incomplete or outdated, so important prospects should be checked before outreach.

How often can scans run?

You can configure a campaign schedule. Actual execution can be affected by provider quotas, API availability, rate limits, errors, and your account configuration.

Will LeadFinder send messages to businesses automatically?

The current product is focused on discovery, organization, exports, and notifications about your scans. You remain responsible for deciding if, when, and how to contact a business.

Is my data public?

Your account and campaign data are protected by authentication and database access controls. You should still avoid putting unnecessary sensitive information into campaign fields or exported files.
